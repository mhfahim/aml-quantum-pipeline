Panel Revision Statistical Significance Summary
================================================

Tests included:
1. McNemar exact test for paired threshold-level prediction disagreement.
2. Paired bootstrap tests for Accuracy, Precision, Recall, F1-score, FPR, ROC-AUC, and PR-AUC.
3. Low Accuracy / High FPR diagnostic table based on confusion matrix results.

Important note: Pairwise tests are performed only inside the same evaluation scope.
No cross-scope comparison is made between full-scale, reduced-subset, and hardware settings.
